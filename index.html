Core Design Pillar

One Battle. Two Minds.

Most strategy games make you choose: the grand view or the tactical duel. BARLOVENTO demands mastery of both across massive multi-front battlefields.

Phase 1 • Strategic Vector Planning

The Approach (Admiral Phase)

Plot simultaneous fleet vectors across dynamic sector charts scaling up to 16×12 grids. Contest Éther Conduits (⚡ +1 AP / +15% Speed), surf Barlovento wind streams, and divide your forces into independent task groups before firing guns.

Phase 2 • 3D Turn-Based Tactics

The Engagement (Ace Phase)

Master Newtonian inertia, orbital momentum, and vector drift in a 3D sphere. Allocate Action Points (AP) for rolling broadsides, beam funnels, and retro-burns while angling sloped armor against kinetic salvos.

Epic Scale • 36 Campaign Stages

Grand Fleet Multi-Front Warfare

Clash in monumental battles commanding up to 15 warships and mobile suits against 16 enemy battle groups. Coordinate center flagships, port flanking wings, starboard harriers, and rear station defenses simultaneously.

Three Ways. One Dying Sea.

Asymmetric Civilizations

The Éther is fading. The Calm spreads across the Star Sea. Three incompatible ideologies contest the last remaining currents.

Steel • Dam the Wind

The Armada of Acero

"The wind is not sacred. It is fuel. When the sea dies, we forge dams of steel."

A hard-science industrial war empire. Massive gunmetal Anvil Dreadnoughts, Hammer Battlecruisers, and the supreme Juggernaut Imperator. Crushing broadside ballistics and triple-battery spinal casemates designed to dominate space through tonnage and unyielding discipline.

Soul • Transcend the Wind

The Vinculados

"Our minds are tuned to the hum of the void. We will transcend before the silence."

Elite mystical pilots neurally linked to the vacuum. Command agile Eidolon, Nous, and Anima Mobile Suit Platoons operating alongside Cradle Carrier Arks and the colossal Seraph-Prime Mobile Armor with golden V-fin crests, 4 seraph wing-binders, and autonomous beam funnels.

Wind • Awaken the Sea

The Free Tide (Marea Libre)

"To bind the wind is to choke it. Unfurl the quantum canvas and ride the storm."

Steampunk nomad corsairs who sail the Éther currents directly. Weathered void-galleons with dark mahogany timbers, burnished brass strakes, glowing mechanical energy oars, and billowing semi-rigid turquoise quantum membrane sails that catch the stellar storm where others drift helpless.

Design Depth

Built for Tactical Purists

⚓

True Newtonian Drift

No magical brakes or instant turns. Every ship fights its own momentum vector. Drift is not a visual effect—it is an active tactical weapon.

✧

3D Sphere & Unit Zoom

Combat unfolds across 3D space with a full free camera. Use [V]/[F] or click any selected unit to zoom seamlessly down to 2.8m for close-up inspection.

➡

The Weather Gauge [Barlovento]

Sail along cyan Éther currents to seize the upwind advantage: +15% hit accuracy and +18% damage on port & starboard broadsides.

⚔

Directional Armor & Arcs

Ships feature localized armor integrity across Bow, Port, Starboard, and Stern quadrants. Angling sloped prow armor absorbs kinetic fire; stern rakes inflict critical breaches.

☁

The Calm Wavefront

A dynamic, encroaching boundary of dead vacuum that expands each campaign turn. Ships caught inside lose wind boost, power, and drift to their graves.

⚙

Secret Developer Console

Press Ctrl+Shift+D anywhere in-game to toggle the Dev Menu: God Mode damage immunity, direct stage jumping across all 36 stages, AP boosts, and win triggers.
