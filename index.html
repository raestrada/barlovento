Core Design Pillar

One Battle. Two Minds.

Most strategy games make you choose: the grand view or the tactical duel. BARLOVENTO demands mastery of both.

Phase 1 • 10×8 Strategic Chart

The Approach (Admiral Phase)

Plot fleet waypoints across 80 quadrants (A1–J8). Contest Éther Conduits (⚡ +1 AP / +15% Speed), surf Barlovento streams (» +15% Acc / +12% DMG), and execute tactical formations while following the 5-step Tactical Academy guide.

Phase 2 • 3D Turn-Based Tactics

The Engagement (Ace Phase)

Master Newtonian inertia and vector drift in a 3D sphere. Allocate Action Points (AP) for broadside salvos, oar bursts, and shields. Mitigate incoming fire with live directional armor integrity across Bow, Flanks, and Stern.

Civilizations • Permadeath Campaign

Three Asymmetric Campaigns

Experience the Star Sea from three unique perspectives. Play guided tutorial stages for The Armada of Acero, The Vinculados, and The Free Tide, developing hero aces with persistent veteran progression.

Three Ways. One Dying Sea.

Asymmetric Civilizations

The Éther is fading. The Calm spreads across the Star Sea. Three incompatible ideologies contest the last remaining currents.

Steel • Dam the Wind

The Armada of Acero

"The wind is not sacred. It is fuel. When the sea dies, we forge dams of steel."

A hard-science industrial war navy. Massive chamfered dreadnoughts, superficial triple-turret batteries, reinforced ramming prows, and crushing broadside ballistics designed to control space through sheer tonnage and discipline.

Soul • Transcend the Wind

The Vinculados

"Our minds are tuned to the hum of the void. We will transcend before the silence."

Elite mystical pilots neurally bonded to the vacuum. Deploy agile 3-mecha Mobile Suit Platoons operating alongside the Cradle Carrier Ark (Pegasus archetype) with white ceramic armor, harmonic crests, pulsing magenta soul cores, resonance barriers, and beam rifles executing lethal stern rakes.

Wind • Awaken the Sea

The Free Tide

"To bind the wind is to choke it. Unfurl the quantum canvas and ride the storm."

Steampunk nomad corsairs who sail the Éther currents directly. Ornate void-caravels with curved mahogany timbers, burnished brass strakes, glowing void-oars, and billowing turquoise quantum sails that gain speed where others drift helpless.

Design Depth

Built for Tactical Purists

⚓

True Newtonian Drift

No magical brakes or instant turns. Every ship fights its own momentum vector. Drift is not a visual effect—it is your greatest tactical weapon.

✧

3D Sphere & Unit Zoom

Combat unfolds across 3D space with a full free camera. Use [V]/[F] or click any selected unit to zoom seamlessly down to 2.8m for close-up inspection.

➡

The Weather Gauge [Barlovento]

Sail along the cyan Éther currents to seize the upwind advantage: +12% hit accuracy and +18% damage on port & starboard broadsides.

⚔

Per-Facing Armor & Arcs

Ships feature localized directional armor integrity, broadside batteries, and fixed weapon cones. Angling vulnerable flanks away from fire decides survival.

☁

The Calm Wavefront

A dynamic, encroaching boundary of dead vacuum that expands each campaign turn. Ships caught inside lose wind boost, power, and drift to their graves.

★

Hero Aces & Roster

Command elite squadron leaders like Admiral Vane, Ace Kaelen-7, and Captain Solano. Veteran aces earn promotions (+HP/+DMG) that persist across stages.
