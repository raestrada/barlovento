# The Vinculados (soul)

> Principle: **Mind**. Faction: **The Vinculados** — transhuman mecha pilots in
> the spirit of *Gundam*. Doctrine: agility and the duel.

This is the deep-dive for the faction summarized in `../DESIGN.md` §7.2.

---

## 1. Summary

The Vinculados are the mystics who became engineers: a scattered order of
pilots who discovered that the Éther field **couples to consciousness**. They
do not burn the wind for energy (that is the Acero) and they do not sail it
(the Free Tide) — they **become** it, one pilot at a time. Their war is not for
resources but for the soul itself.

## 2. History: the first Voice

- Origin: a fading, dying world whose people, at the edge of extinction, began
  to *hear* the Éther field as a kind of voice. What the Acero measured as
  gradients, the Vinculados experienced as **presence**.
- They learned to answer the voice. The result was the **Link**: a neural
  coupling between a pilot and a machine, enabled by the field. When linked,
  pilot and frame are one — reaction at the speed of thought, no lag between
  will and motion.
- They seeded themselves across the Star Sea not as colonies but as
  **Cradles**: mobile monasteries-carriers that raise pilots and house frames.
  They are a people who live *between* worlds, carried by the sea they listen
  to.

## 3. Society and government

- The governing institution is **the Choir** — a council of the oldest and
  most-attuned pilots, the "voices". Rank is measured not in rank insignia but
  in **resonance**: how fully one hears the field.
- Culture is ascetic and transhuman. The body is a vessel, not an identity; the
  frame is a truer body. A pilot who dies in the Link is said to have **joined
  the Choir**.
- Their doctrine of the sea is the mirror image of the Acero's: the sea is
  **alive and conscious**, and to burn it (as Acero does) is a kind of
  cannibalism. This is the ideological fault line between the two.

## 4. The Link and the soul

- The Link is the faction's heart, and the source of its mechanical identity:
  linked pilots fight at **superhuman tempo** — extra actions, drift control at
  high speed, a frame that moves as an extension of the self.
- The Link is also their vulnerability. It requires **resonance** — a healthy,
  close coupling to the field. Where the field thins, the Link fails.
- **"Cold"** is what a Vinculado becomes when the Link breaks: pilot and
  machine separate, the frame goes sluggish and stupid, and the pilot is left
  alone inside their own skull — a living death for a people defined by union.

## 5. Technology

- **Frames** (mobile suits) are their primary vessels: sleek humanoid machines
  that read the pilot's nervous system directly through the Link.
- **Cradles** are carriers that also serve as temples and creches — they
  sustain the pilots and broadcast resonance to keep links healthy.
- **The Chorus** is their autonomous arm: unpiloted drones commanded through
  the Link by a Conductor hero.
- **Propulsion:** reactionless in spirit — frames move with the same
  drift-and-burn physics as everyone else, but their mass is low and their
  control is unmatched. They do not need much energy because they are so
  little material.

## 6. Doctrine: agility and the duel

- The Vinculados fight as **individuals, not formations**. Where the Acero
  commit to a line, a Vinculados squad dissolves into a dance of feints and
  duels.
- **Inertia profile:** the lowest mass and the highest agility in the game.
  They are the only faction that can truly *react* — turn on a vector, exploit
  drift, slip through firing arcs.
- The cost: they cannot trade broadsides. A frame that stands still dies. The
  skill ceiling is the highest in the game, and so is the floor.

### Blurbs

- **Eidolon** — the frame a great pilot grows into; a mirror of the soul that
  flies it. No two are alike.
- **Nous** — the reasoned fist: heavy enough to crack a shield, precise enough
  to do it in one pass.
- **Anima** — pure speed; a strike that is gone before the shot lands.
- **Psyche** — the eyes of the squad; scout, bait, and the first to see the
  opening.
- **Thumos** — a frame built for the fury of close combat; a duelist's joy and
  a commander's gamble.
- **Logos** — carries the electronic-warfare choir; it sings the enemy's
  sensors to sleep and wakes its own allies' Links.
- **Cradle** — the mothership. Lose the Cradle and the squad loses its
  resonance, its repair, and its home.

## 8. Hero types (the aces)

| Hero | Archetype | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Linked Ace** | Frame pilot | The duelist — high ceiling, personal combat |
| **Conductor** | Support / EW | Commands the Chorus, buffs linked allies |
| **Ascendant** | Transcendent veteran | High power, burns toward the Ascension |

- **Linked Ace** — the Gundam pilot fantasy: superhuman tempo, drift mastery,
  a single pilot who can turn a fight. The emotional core of the faction.
- **Conductor** — less a fighter, more a mind: amplifies allies' Links, steers
  the Chorus, and degrades enemy sensors. The tactical multiplier.
- **Ascendant** — a pilot who has begun to transcend; immensely powerful, but
  every fight pushes them closer to leaving the body — a hero you may *lose to
  victory*.

## 9. Gameplay identity (mechanics)

- **Admiral phase:** weakest in formation terms. Their strength is
  *positioning the duel* — where each frame enters the fight, and from what
  vector. They trade formation initiative for **ambush** and angle.
- **Ace phase:** the faction's home. High actions, low inertia, the dance of
  drift and feint. The ceiling is enormous; the floor is cruel.
- **The Link mechanic:** linked frames gain tempo bonuses; when resonance is
  low (near the Calm, or after a Cradle is damaged) they fight **cold** and
  lose the bonuses that define them.
- **Resource:** **resonance** — field attunement harvested and sustained by
  Cradles. It powers Links and frames. As the Calm advances, resonance thins,
  and the Vinculados grow colder the closer they press to the front.

## 10. The Calm and the Ascension

- The Calm does not starve the Vinculados; it **deafens** them. As the field
  dies, the voice they built their identity around goes silent, and their
  pilots fight cold.
- **The Ascension** is their answer: a rite to shed the body and become pure
  consciousness in the field, leaving the dying sea behind entirely.
- The tragic loop: every Ascension **punctures the field**, thinning it
  further. Their salvation and their sin are the same act. They may be the very
  reason the wind is dying.
- **Victory (war goal):** complete the Ascension **before** the Calm covers
  them — transcend the sea, whatever that costs everyone else.

## 11. Visual identity

Sleek, organic-curved, humanoid frames. White ceramic armor, dark sinew
joints, a glowing core; cold magenta/violet for the soul-link energy. Carriers
are secondary; the frame is the icon. See
[`../art/prompts/factions/vinculados.md`](../art/prompts/factions/vinculados.md)
for the canonical prompt tokens.

## 12. Mood and audio

Elegant, transhuman, slightly uncanny. The sound of a Vinculados squad is
near-silence broken by precise motion and a faint harmonic hum — the Choir,
singing under the noise of battle.
