# BARLOVENTO — Dialogue Bible & Voice Direction Guide

> **Purpose:** Standardizes dialogue writing, voice acting delivery, radio chatter protocols, and terminology across the three campaigns.  
> **Key Reference:** The understated, documentary-style military realism of *Homeworld* and *Battlestar Galactica*, contrasted with the mythic operatic intensity of *Mobile Suit Gundam*.

---

## 1. General Dialogue Philosophy

1. **Subtext and Brevity Over Exposition:** Characters do not explain the world to one another; they live in it. Technical terms, spatial coordinates, and naval jargon are delivered naturally without hand-holding.
2. **Environmental Physicality:** Radio transmissions carry the acoustic reality of the speaker’s cockpit or bridge:
   - Acero comms feature heavy engine vibration hum, air recyclers, and muffled hydraulic clanks.
   - Vinculados comms have a faint ethereal resonance, binaural echoes, and subtle psychic harmonics.
   - Free Tide comms are textured with ambient timber stress creaks, whistling wind-field sensors, and lively background crew calls.
3. **The "Homeworld Rule" for Radio Chatter:** During fleet-level combat, fleet controllers and captains maintain an icy, disciplined calm even in the face of catastrophic hull breaches. Panic is expressed through rapid breathing, clipped speech, and micro-pauses, never melodramatic screaming.

## 2. Voice Direction & Speech Patterns by Faction

### 2.1 The Armada of Acero
- **Tone:** Austere, formal, hierarchical, laconic. British/Mid-Atlantic naval officer cadence, hard consonants.
- **Form of Address:** Titles, ranks, ship pennant numbers (*"Line-Lead Vanguard"*, *"Admiral on the Deck"*, *"Chisel Three-Zero"*).
- **Core Catchphrases & Idioms:**
  - *"Hold the bearing."*
  - *"Steel endures."*
  - *"Check fire, clear the line."*
  - *"Acknowledge and execute."*

#### Sample Script Excerpt (Fleet Control & Ace Pilot):
```
FLEET TACTICAL (COMMS)
All elements, Admiral on the link. Formation Anvil-Seven confirmed. Approach burn is five seconds on our mark... Three, two, one, execute.

LANCER-ONE (VESPER CRUZ)
Thrust positive. Vector aligned at mark one-eight-zero. Lead, we’ve got hostile signatures rolling out of the wake. Reading three corsair brigs bearing leeward.

FLEET TACTICAL (COMMS)
Understood, Lancer-One. Maintain screen. Do not break formation to chase. Let the Crucible broadsides take the angle.

LANCER-ONE (VESPER CRUZ)
Copy, Command. Holding the line. But tell the Crucible gunners to watch their drift—they’re crowding my firing lane.
```

---

### 2.2 The Vinculados
- **Tone:** Intimate, reverent, melancholic, heightened awareness. Soft, measured delivery with poetic and philosophical imagery.
- **Form of Address:** Monastic names, harmonic titles (*"First Voice"*, *"Conductor Sola"*, *"Brother Kaelen"*).
- **The "Cold State" Vocal Effect:** When a pilot is in a dead-wind or low-resonance zone, their voice loses all melodic resonance, sounding dry, flat, and claustrophobic.
- **Core Catchphrases & Idioms:**
  - *"The void remembers."*
  - *"Listen to the curve."*
  - *"Resonance confirmed; I am the needle."*
  - *"They fight deaf and blind."*

#### Sample Script Excerpt (Linked Frame Ace in Combat):
```
CONDUCTOR SOLA (TELEPATHIC CARRIER LINK)
Kaelen. The choir in the fourth quadrant has thinned. Can you hear the gap?

EIDOLON PRIME (KAELEN-7)
(Whispering, breathing synchronized with thruster pulses)
I feel it, Mother. It is cold. Like someone cut the silver cord.

CONDUCTOR SOLA
Then be the warmth. Burn your reaction mass. Intersect their vector before the Anvil turns its gaze upon the Cradle.

EIDOLON PRIME (KAELEN-7)
Frame responding. My thoughts are in the nozzles. I see the seam in their armor... Diving now. For the quiet ones who went before.
```

---

### 2.3 The Free Tide (*La Marea Libre*)
- **Tone:** Warm, passionate, roguish, familial, defying death with laughter and sea shanties. Mediterranean/Latin cadence, rolling rhythms.
- **Form of Address:** Informal ranks, affectionate nicknames, terms of endearment (*"Capitán"*, *"Hermano"*, *"Windrider"*, *"Patrón"*).
- **Core Catchphrases & Idioms:**
  - *"Good wind and clear sea."*
  - *"Trim the sheets!"*
  - *"Let them eat our wake."*
  - *"Hold to the spar, lads!"*

#### Sample Script Excerpt (Bridge of *El Albatros Dorado*):
```
CAPTAIN MATEO BRISA
(Laughing against the rumble of the hull)
Look at that Acero line! Heavy, fat, and moving like a frozen bull! Silvia, what’s our water reading?

SILVIA (HARPOON BRIGANTINE)
Current is picking up from the north rim, Capitán! Three knots of éteron drift and rising!

CAPTAIN MATEO BRISA
Unfurl the outer skysails! Drop the helm ten degrees into the trough! We’re going to cross their bows before their gunners can even calculate the parallax.

OLD MATEO (WIND CALLER)
(Gravelly, contemplative)
Gently with the canvas, boy. The sea is tired today. She gives us this breath, but do not ask her for a storm.
```

## 3. Standard In-Game Combat Bark Taxonomy

| Trigger Event | Acero Line Bark | Vinculados Frame Bark | Free Tide Corsair Bark |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Approach Order Placed** | *"Vector plotted. Engines committing to the burn."* | *"Concurrence achieved. The frame flows forward."* | *"Sheets trimmed! We’re on the wind!"* |
| **Broadside Fired** | *"Main battery salvo away. Impact in six seconds."* | *"Sending the light. Severing their alignment."* | *"Give 'em all guns! Let the brass sing!"* |
| **Shield/Armor Critical** | *"Bow armor compromised. Bulkhead three venting."* | *"The link is bleeding... my skin burns."* | *"Planking splintered! Bilge pumps to full!"* |
| **Kill Confirmed** | *"Target neutralized. Scrubbed from sensor grid."* | *"Their voice is silenced. May they find peace."* | *"Down she goes to the black deep! Cheers, lads!"* |
| **Entering Calm Zone** | *"Warning: Éteron gradient lost. Reactor fuel load tripled."* | *"No... the silence... I can't feel my hands..."* | *"Becalmed! Sails slack! Man the field oars!"* |
