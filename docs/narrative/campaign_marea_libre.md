# Campaign: The Free Tide (*La Marea Libre*) — *"The Breath of the Void"* (*El Aliento del Vacío*)

> **Faction:** The Free Tide (Spirit)  
> **Length:** 12 Missions (Full Narrative Campaign)  
> **Difficulty Arc:** Fluid Positioning Mastery, High-Drift Inertia, Boarding & Subsystem Sabotage, Weather-Gauge Exploitation  
> **Key References:** *Porco Rosso* & *Treasure Planet* (romantic, seafaring space adventurism), *Master and Commander* (naval age-of-sail tactics, weather gauge, broadside duels), *Homeworld: Kharak/Deserts* (the desperate cross-sea expedition).

---

## Campaign Summary & Narrative Stakes

The Free Tide (*La Marea Libre*) have never owned a homeworld. They are nomadic void-sailors whose brass-and-ironwood galleons fly on gossamer sails that catch the dark-energy currents of the Star Sea. When the Calm begins to spread, it brings the ultimate naval horror: **Becalming** (*La Encalmada*). A becalmed corsair ship has no reactor to fall back on; when the wind stops, the ship dies in the water, its crew doomed to suffocate in the black deep.

Led by the charismatic **Captain Mateo "Brisa" Solano** aboard his legendary three-masted flagship, *El Albatros Dorado*, the corsairs refuse to accept a silent death. Joined by the fierce boarding master **Silvia the Harpooner** and the blind, mystic shaman **"El Abuelo"**, Mateo embarks on a desperate odyssey: to find the mythical **Nadir Well (*El Ojo del Viento*)**, the primordial font from which the Star Sea breathes, and rekindle the dying wind before the entire universe is frozen in silence.

## Mission Roster & Tactical Breakdowns

### Mission 01: *Becalmed*
- **Location:** The Stagnant Deep (Old Trading Lane).
- **Starting Flotilla:**
  - 1x *Windjammer*-class Flagship (*El Albatros Dorado*, Mateo)
  - 1x *Brigantine*-class Raider (*La Navaja*, Silvia)
  - 1x *Barque*-class Workhorse (*Resuello*, El Abuelo)
- **Primary Objective:** Man the emergency steam-oars to tow two stranded civilian caravels across a dead-wind pocket to the nearest active current.
- **Secondary Objective:** Siphon emergency boiler coal/fuel from three drifting trade wrecks (+300 Steam Reserves).
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The mission starts in a **Dead Zone**. Sail thrust is zero. Ships must expend scarce steam reserves or deploy rowing crews (éther-oars) to maneuver at slow speed.
- **Scripted Ambush:** An Acero salvage patrol arrives to scuttle the stranded ships for scrap iron. Silvia executes a desperate boarding action, swinging across the void on magnetic tethers to seize an enemy corvette.

---

### Mission 02: *Catching the Trade Winds*
- **Location:** The Great Zephyr Stream.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla + captured Acero corvette.
- **Primary Objective:** Raid an Acero logistics convoy while riding the 40-knot hyper-current.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The current stream grants +200% forward speed and +3 drift momentum along the flow vector. Acero heavy dreadnoughts struggle to turn against the current, allowing Free Tide brigantines to execute deadly hit-and-fade passes.
- **Narrative Loot:** The convoy yields star charts showing the location of Acero's Great Sluice facilities and the mysterious energy drain occurring at the galactic core.

---

### Mission 03: *The Corsair Parley*
- **Location:** The Smuggler's Haven of *Puerto Libre* (Hollowed Asteroid Citadelle).
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Defeat the rebellious clan leader "Iron Hook" Roberto in a flagship duel to unite the pirate clans under Mateo’s banner.
- **Secondary Objective:** Disarm Roberto’s flagship (*El Kraken*) without sinking it.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Close-quarters combat inside a cavernous hollow asteroid. Rigging collisions, sail-snagging, and boarding broadsides.
- **Atmospheric Moment:** After the duel, the entire corsair haven erupts into a traditional void-shanty (*"El Viento es Nuestro Padre"*), swearing allegiance to the expedition toward the Nadir Well.

---

### Mission 04: *Boarding the Behemoth*
- **Location:** The Iron Gateway.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla + 2x *Skiff* Boarding Cutters.
- **Primary Objective:** Grapple and board the Acero Heavy Battlecruiser *Goliath*, capture the navigational crypts, and scuttle its reactor.
- **Secondary Objective:** Silvia's boarding team must secure the enemy bridge within 6 tactical turns.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Boarding mechanics: The player must shoot away the target's flak turrets, maneuver to within grappling range, launch magnetic cables, and transfer boarding units across the void while under fire.

---

### Mission 05: *Flight Through the Needles*
- **Location:** The Spire Reef (Dense Crystalline Asteroid Belt).
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla + 1x *Galleon*-class Heavy Broadside Ship (*La Santa Trinidad*).
- **Primary Objective:** Navigate through the jagged crystalline spires while pursued by a squadron of Vinculado hunter-killer frames.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Narrow corridors where speed and drift control are everything. The player must use tacking maneuvers to rebound momentum off gravitational vortices. Vinculado frames struggle with tight obstacle navigation due to their delicate ceramic wings.

---

### Mission 06: *The Shaman's Song*
- **Location:** The Shrouded Shallows.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Protect *Resuello* as El Abuelo conducts the ancient ritual to coax a dormant éteron current back to life.
- **Secondary Objective:** Defeat the incoming Acero strike wing without allowing any missile to hit the ritual barque.
- **Hero Mechanics:** El Abuelo uses his unique **Windcaller** skill: for 3 turns, the dead-zone around the ship bursts with luminous cyan currents, granting all Free Tide ships maximum combat agility and blowing enemy torpedoes off course.

---

### Mission 07: *The Fall of Puerto Libre*
- **Location:** High Orbit of Asteroid *Puerto Libre*.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Full Corsair Armada (All persistent ships + volunteer privateers).
- **Primary Objective:** Hold off an overwhelming Acero siege armada while the civilian refugee flotilla cuts their moorings and unfurls sails.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Defensive fleet siege. Sacrificing light caravels to draw dreadnought broadsides away from slow-moving hospital galleons.
- **Tragic Resolution:** Puerto Libre is pulverized by Acero orbital mass-drivers. Mateo watches the pirate citadel shatter into dust: *"We are homeless now, lads. From this moment on, the wind is our only soil."*

---

### Mission 08: *Ghost Fleets*
- **Location:** The Sargasso of Stars.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Salvage the legendary pre-Concord flagship *Nuestra Señora de los Vientos* from a cursed ship graveyard.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The battlefield is packed with hundreds of derelict wooden hulks drifting in stagnant éteron pools. Navigating through the hulks requires precision bow thruster maneuvers.
- **Narrative Discovery:** The crew recovers the **Grand Spinnaker**—a mythical sail membrane woven from primordial éteron filaments that can catch currents even in the deepest Calm.

---

### Mission 09: *Breaker’s Run*
- **Location:** The Frontline of the Calm.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Race across an advancing Calm wall to enter the deep hyper-current leading to the galactic core.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The Calm wavefront sweeps across the map at high velocity. Ships must ride the turbulent slipstream immediately ahead of the wave. Dropping out of the current means instant immobility and death.

---

### Mission 10: *Strike on the Sluice*
- **Location:** Sluice Station Omega (Flank Sector).
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Breaching the outer magnetic containment locks of Acero’s primary dam station to liberate trapped currents.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Sabotage operation. Free Tide skiffs slip into the intake valves while the heavy galleons trade thunderous broadsides with Acero defense monitors.
- **Cinematic Release:** The locks shatter. Millions of tons of compressed, pent-up dark energy erupt into the void, forming a roaring current that hurls Mateo’s fleet directly toward the Nadir Well.

---

### Mission 11: *The Convergence* (The Three Fleets)
- **Location:** The Outer Rim of the Nadir Well.
- **Starting Flotilla:** Persistent Flotilla.
- **Primary Objective:** Survive a chaotic three-way engagement between the desperate remnants of Acero’s 4th Fleet, dying Vinculado Ascendant zealots, and your corsair armada.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** True 3-way naval chaos. Environmental currents churn in a violent spiral toward the central maelstrom. Alliances shift turn by turn as ships are pulled toward the vortex.

---

### Mission 12: *The Heart of the Hurricane* (Campaign Climax)
- **Location:** The Eye of the Wind (Nadir Well Core).
- **Starting Flotilla:** Mateo's Flagship *El Albatros Dorado*, Silvia's *La Navaja*, El Abuelo's *Resuello*, plus elite escorts.
- **Primary Objective:** Unfurl the Grand Spinnaker at the exact center of the vortex while defending against the cosmic entity of the Calm.
- **Climactic Resolution:**
  - El Abuelo sacrifices his sight and life-force to channel the final prayer into the sea.
  - Silvia executes a daring grapple run onto the collapsing core structure to clear the sail boom.
  - Mateo unfurls the Grand Spinnaker: the membrane catches the primordial spark. A deafening, glorious wind roars across the void, sending a shockwave of reawakened quintessence across the galaxy.
  - The Calm recedes. The sails billow once more. The Star Sea is reborn, free, wild, and open to all who dare to ride the wind.
