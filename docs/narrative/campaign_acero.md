# Campaign: The Armada of Acero — *"The Iron Sluice"* (*El Dique de Hierro*)

> **Faction:** The Armada of Acero (Body)  
> **Length:** 12 Missions (Full Narrative Campaign)  
> **Difficulty Arc:** Military Realism, Severe Resource Attrition, Homeworld-Grade Fleet Persistence  
> **Key References:** *Homeworld*, *Battlestar Galactica: Deadlock*, *Master and Commander*

---

## Campaign Summary & Narrative Stakes

High Admiral Valerius Vance commands the 4th Fleet of the Acero Admiralty. His mandate from the Grand Assembly at *Anvil* is unequivocal: **Guarantee the Éteron logistics lifeline at any cost**, protect the construction of the colossal engineering station known as **The Great Sluice (*La Gran Compuerta*)**, and crush any resistance from Free Tide corsairs or Vinculado zealots.

As the Calm begins consuming frontier wells, Vance is forced into brutal moral compromises: abandoning civilian colonies, cannibalizing damaged line vessels, and violently suppressing mutinies, only to discover that the Sluice itself may be triggering the very extinction event he was ordered to prevent.

## Mission Roster & Tactical Breakdowns

### Mission 01: *Sounding the Well*
- **Location:** Well Station 09, The Rust Shoals (Border Marches).
- **Starting Fleet:** 
  - 1x *Crucible*-class Cruiser (*Indomitable*)
  - 2x *Dirk*-class Escort Frigates (*Talon*, *Rake*)
  - 1x Strike Wing (*Lancer-One*, Flight Lt. Vesper Cruz)
- **Primary Objective:** Escort two super-heavy *Goliath* Ore Barges to the perimeter jump gate.
- **Secondary Objective:** Protect both barges with zero hull breaches (+500 Refined Éteron bonus).
- **Inertia & Environmental Terrain:** Low-density debris ring; ships moving faster than speed 4 suffer collision risks against asteroid scrap.
- **Scripted Narrative Beats:**
  - *T+2 Minutes:* Well Station 09 stops transmitting mid-broadcast. Sensor logs show the ambient éteron gradient plunging to zero.
  - *T+4 Minutes:* A wolfpack of Free Tide raiders jumps from behind a hollowed asteroid, targeting the vulnerable civilian barges.
  - *Vesper Cruz Dialogue:* *"Command, those aren't pirate colours. Those are refugee markings on those corsair brigs. They're trying to syphon fuel, not sink them!"* Vance responds: *"We do not negotiate logistics, Lieutenant. Fire on the lead hull."*
- **Debriefing & Persistence:** Surviving hulls carry over into Mission 02. Barges deliver raw Éteron used for early repair yards.

---

### Mission 02: *Crossing the Shoals*
- **Location:** The Gorgon Narrows.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet + 1x *Hammer*-class Battlecruiser (*Iron Duke*).
- **Primary Objective:** Sweep the shoals of corsair ambush craft and establish a secure perimeter for the survey flotilla.
- **Secondary Objective:** Force the flagship of Corsair Captain Teresa to surrender instead of destroying it.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Narrow spatial corridor. Crossing the T is essential: if Acero ships turn broadsides too early, drifting cross-currents push them into minefields.
- **Key Scripted Event:** Acero long-range sensors detect an unidentified anomalous signal—a high-frequency violet harmonic pulse echoing from deep within Vinculado space.

---

### Mission 03: *Hold the Breach*
- **Location:** Perimeter Bastion Kelvin-4.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Hold Kelvin-4 against waves of high-speed Vinculado combat frames for 8 turns until evacuation shuttles depart.
- **Secondary Objective:** Shoot down the Vinculado Ace Frame *Anima-Zero* with Vesper Cruz's strike fighter.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** First encounter with extreme-agility mobile suits. Acero flak screens (*Dirk* frigates) must be layered tightly to prevent frames from slipping into the dead blind-spots beneath the capital ships' ventral guns.
- **Narrative Reveal:** Black box data salvaged from a downed frame reveals the Vinculados were not raiding for fuel—they were conducting a metaphysical calibration ritual that tore open a spatial fault.

---

### Mission 04: *The Sluice Anchor*
- **Location:** Orbital Frame of the Great Sluice, Core Sector Anvil-Prime.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet + 1x *Foundry*-class Fleet Carrier (*Hephaestus*).
- **Primary Objective:** Protect the 4 Anchor Pylons of the Sluice while Chief Engineer Ruiz initiates the harmonic field compressors.
- **Secondary Objective:** Destroy the incoming torpedo bombers before any anchor takes more than 25% damage.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The Sluice creates an artificial directional current. Units aligned with the compressor gain +50% speed; units fighting against it suffer double engine strain.
- **Narrative Climax:** The Sluice engages. A vast, glowing compression field stabilizes local space. Instantly, sensor feeds from the neighboring system (*Caelum*) go dark: the entire system has been prematurely plunged into the Calm by the back-draft.

---

### Mission 05: *Cold Iron*
- **Location:** Dead Sector Caelum.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Search the abandoned mining colony for stranded tankers; extract remaining éteron fuel before life support fails.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** **Full Calm Zone.** Ambient gradient = 0. All ships consume 300% standard fuel to maneuver. There is zero drift assistance. Stopping requires hard retrograde burns.
- **Horror Narrative:** Derelict Acero transports drift silently. The crews froze in their bunks when the reactors starved. Cruz's cockpit audio records the creak of freezing armor and the despair of the fleet controllers.

---

### Mission 06: *The Anvil Strikes*
- **Location:** Sanctuary Outer Rim, Monastery World *Sanctum-Four*.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet + 1x *Anvil*-class Dreadnought (*Vance's Flagship: Aegis of Anvil*).
- **Primary Objective:** Shatter the perimeter defense grid and capture the Vinculado Cradle station *Aeterna*.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Heavy dreadnought warfare. The *Aegis of Anvil* can absorb colossal frontal fire, but its turning circle is enormous. Escort corvettes must screen against swarm torpedoes.
- **Narrative Turning Point:** Inside the Cradle, Acero boarding teams find no military installations—only thousands of pilots in meditative stasis cradles, chanting in agony as the Calm deadens their minds.

---

### Mission 07: *The Leeward Ambush*
- **Location:** The Saffron Maelstrom.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Escort the damaged *Aegis of Anvil* out of an asteroid hurricane while ambushed by Free Tide heavy galleons.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Volatile currents change vector every 2 turns. Free Tide galleons ride the surges to execute brutal point-blank broadsides.
- **Character Conflict:** Chief Engineer Ruiz informs Vance that the mathematical models prove the Sluice is accelerating the collapse of the universal field. Vance orders her arrested for treason: *"The Sluice is the only thing standing between our people and the dark. The line holds."*

---

### Mission 08: *Scorched Void*
- **Location:** Forge Colony *Ferrum-Beta*.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Evacuate essential industrial engineers and fire scuttling charges into the orbital foundry before the advancing Calm wavefront sweeps the planet.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The Calm wavefront is physically modeled on the tactical grid: it advances 2 grid hexes per turn from the west edge. Any ship caught behind the wave is permanently lost.
- **Emotional Weight:** Civilian transports beg for escort slots. The player has only enough fleet command capacity to save a fraction of the convoys.

---

### Mission 09: *Trial of Steel*
- **Location:** Staging Base Castor.
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Put down a mutiny led by Admiral Katherine Vance (Valerius Vance's estranged sister), who has defected to protect civilian refugee fleets.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Mirror battle. Both sides field identical Acero doctrine (dreadnoughts, battlecruisers, flak screens). The player must out-maneuver disciplined line formations and cross the enemy's T.
- **Tragic Resolution:** Vance fires the killing broadside into his sister's battlecruiser. Cruz refuses an order to strafe surviving escape pods, marking the breaking point of her loyalty.

---

### Mission 10: *The Shattered Conduit*
- **Location:** The Great Sluice Backbone (Outer Ring).
- **Starting Fleet:** Persistent Fleet.
- **Primary Objective:** Clear saboteurs from the primary turbine conduits and restart coolant pumps before reactor core meltdown.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Trench warfare in 3D: ships navigate massive open structural bays inside the megastructure. Strike fighters have high freedom of motion; capital ships can only fire down aligned structural corridors.
- **Scripted Hero Moment:** Cruz disobeys flight safety protocols, diving her strike craft into an exhaust flume to destroy a corsair detonator array.

---

### Mission 11: *The Wall Holds*
- **Location:** High Orbit above *Anvil* (Homeworld).
- **Starting Fleet:** Full Acero Grand Fleet (All surviving persistent units + planetary defense monitors).
- **Primary Objective:** Defend the orbital defense ring against a desperate joint assault by dying Free Tide armadas and deranged Vinculado renegades.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Massive fleet clash. Over 40 combat hulls on screen simultaneously. Formation coherence provides fleet-wide accuracy bonuses; maintaining line discipline is mandatory to survive the sheer volume of enemy fire.

---

### Mission 12: *The Empty Sea* (Campaign Climax)
- **Location:** The Core Sluice Hub at the Edge of the Calm.
- **Starting Fleet:** Player Fleet Core.
- **Primary Objective:** Power the final seal on the Great Sluice to create a permanent fortified sanctuary around the Acero core worlds.
- **Climactic Choice & Resolution:**
  - The Sluice reaches critical charge. Ruiz reveals over comms that firing it will wall off the Acero sector, but the resulting shockwave will permanently extinguish the remaining currents across the entire rest of the Star Sea, condemning the other civilizations to instant death in the Calm.
  - Vance gives the order: *"Let history judge us in the warmth of our forge fires. Seal the gate."*
  - The campaign ends with the Great Sluice firing: a blind wall of artificial force locks down space. Inside, Acero endures, isolated, silent, and surrounded by an infinite, dead sea.
