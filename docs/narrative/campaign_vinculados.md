# Campaign: The Vinculados — *"The Last Choir"* (*El Último Coro*)

> **Faction:** The Vinculados (Mind)  
> **Length:** 12 Missions (Full Narrative Campaign)  
> **Difficulty Arc:** High Skill Ceiling, Agility Mastery, Neural Resonance Resource Loop, Pilot Permadeath/Cold-Severing  
> **Key References:** *Mobile Suit Gundam* (Newtype tragedy and personal mobile suit duels), *Neon Genesis Evangelion* (neural synchronization and psychological vulnerability), *Homeworld: Cataclysm* (existential dread in the deep void).

---

## Campaign Summary & Narrative Stakes

The Vinculados do not view space as territory, but as a living cathedral of thought. To them, the **Link** is not an instrument of war—it is communion. A pilot linked to an *Eidolon* or *Anima* frame does not steer thrusters; they *feel* the vacuum against their own nerves.

When the Calm arrives, it is not merely a loss of energy—it is **universal sensory deprivation**. The field goes silent, and pilots who lose resonance are left trapped in total sensory isolation ("The Cold"). Led by the enigmatic **Conductor Sola** from the monastery-carrier *Cradle of Resonances*, the elite ace **Kaelen-7** must escort the pilgrimage toward the mythical **Ascension Gate**. But as veteran pilot **Mirei** begins her irreversible transformation into an Ascendant entity, Kaelen uncovers a devastating truth: the Ascension does not heal the universe—it cannibalizes the remaining Éther to forge a doorway out of this dying cosmos.

## Mission Roster & Tactical Breakdowns

### Mission 01: *The Severed Chord*
- **Location:** The Outer Veil, Sector Psalm-4.
- **Starting Squad:**
  - 1x *Cradle*-class Carrier (*Cradle of Resonances*, Command Hub)
  - 1x *Eidolon*-class Command Frame (*Eidolon Prime*, Ace Kaelen-7)
  - 2x *Anima*-class Strike Frames (*Aria*, *Stanza*)
- **Primary Objective:** Intercept and disable an Acero industrial harvester fleet that is forcibly siphoning a sacred resonance well.
- **Secondary Objective:** Execute precision subsystem strikes on the harvester boilers without destroying the crew compartments.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** First exposure to 3D vector mecha movement. Frames can perform lateral drift burns and slip through weapon arcs with zero inertia lag.
- **Scripted Horror Event:** Halfway through the battle, a sudden micro-burst of the Calm sweeps the sector. *Stanza*'s pilot screams as the Link flatlines. The frame's thrusters lock up, and the unit goes permanently Cold.

---

### Mission 02: *Echoes in the Mist*
- **Location:** The Amethyst Nebula.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad + 1x *Logos*-class EW Support Frame (*Logos-V*).
- **Primary Objective:** Infiltrate an Acero radar sensor outpost using the *Logos* frame to mask the *Cradle*'s psychic emission footprint.
- **Secondary Objective:** Hack the outpost core databanks to extract navigational charts for the Ascension Gate.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Stealth and electronic warfare. The player must project harmonic jamming spheres to conceal moving frames while navigating volatile gaseous clouds that ignite under thruster burns.

---

### Mission 03: *The Silent Cradle*
- **Location:** The Verge of Stillness.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad + 1x *Nous*-class Heavy Assault Frame.
- **Primary Objective:** Protect the sister Cradle *Vespers* while its wounded choir transfers surviving pilots into your carriers.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Low Resonance Zone ($r\text{dB} < 30$). All frames suffer a -30% action point penalty unless tethered within the *Cradle of Resonances*' aura. Acero boarding cutters attempt to breach the sister ship.
- **Audio Design Feature:** Combat music is stripped of mid and high frequencies, replaced by muffled thuds and Kaelen’s panicked, echoing heartbeat.

---

### Mission 04: *Duels of Honor*
- **Location:** The Gilded Current.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad.
- **Primary Objective:** Drive off a flotilla of Free Tide corsair raiders who have claimed the only active current corridor.
- **Secondary Objective:** Defeat Corsair Champion Silvia in single combat without allowing her to grapple your frame.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** High-speed dogfight. Free Tide ships ride the current with immense drift speed, while Vinculado frames utilize instantaneous vector changes to out-angle them.
- **Dialogue Exchange:** Silvia shouts over an open frequency: *"You cold-blooded ghosts! You're draining the sea dry with your prayers!"* Kaelen answers: *"We pray because the sea is weeping, pirate. Step aside, or be silenced."*

---

### Mission 05: *Fracture*
- **Location:** The Maw of Chronos (Gravity Well of a Dead Pulsar).
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad + 1x *Thumos*-class Berserker Frame (*Piloted by Veteran Mirei*).
- **Primary Objective:** Secure three ancient resonant obelisks drifting around the pulsar accretion disk.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Extreme gravitational pull toward the map center each turn. Mirei demonstrates Tier-3 Ascendant abilities, destroying capital-class escorts in a single turn with devastating psychic resonance surges.
- **Narrative Warning:** After the battle, Mirei's telemetry reveals that her biological organs are beginning to crystallize into éteron lattices. She is shedding her humanity.

---

### Mission 06: *The Cold Front*
- **Location:** The Dying Drift.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad.
- **Primary Objective:** Evacuate through an advancing wall of the Calm while defending civilian pilgrimage shuttles.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** The Calm wavefront physically rolls across the battlefield. Units trapped in the wake lose all shield shields and turn-based initiative.
- **Atmospheric Impact:** The player must make agonizing choices: burning limited resonance reserves to pull stranded allied frames out of the dead zone, or preserving power for the *Cradle*.

---

### Mission 07: *The Acero Siphon*
- **Location:** Sluice Relay Station Delta-9.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad + 1x *Chorus* Autonomous Drone Swarm.
- **Primary Objective:** Assault and dismantle the massive compression turbines of an Acero Sluice facility.
- **Secondary Objective:** Overload the facility’s capacitor grid to trigger a reverse shockwave that restores local field flow.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Industrial siege. Acero *Hammer* battlecruisers form a defensive wall. Conductor Sola commands the *Chorus* swarm to peel off escort flak while Kaelen executes a high-speed vector run down the turbine trench.

---

### Mission 08: *Symphony of Iron*
- **Location:** The Obsidian Expanse.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad.
- **Primary Objective:** Engage and neutralize an Acero hunter-killer strike group sent specifically to execute Conductor Sola.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Battle of attrition. Acero utilizes heavy electronic dampening missiles that disrupt frame links. Players must use *Logos* cleansing pulses to restore fallen pilots before they go permanently Cold.
- **Narrative Revelation:** Intercepted logs prove that Acero's High Admiral Vance knows the Sluice accelerates the Calm, but considers total destruction of the other factions an acceptable price for Acero’s survival.

---

### Mission 09: *The Heretic Frame*
- **Location:** The Sunken Cathedral (Derelict Orbital Monastery).
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad.
- **Primary Objective:** Hunt down and confront former First Voice Orestes, who has betrayed the Choir and formed an unholy alliance with Free Tide outcasts.
- **Secondary Objective:** Subdue Orestes’ frame (*Eidolon Umbra*) without destroying the sacred databanks aboard his flagship.
- **Boss Fight Mechanics:** Orestes fields a corrupted frame capable of projecting "Dissonance Fields" that invert the player's controls and disable psychic abilities.
- **Narrative Twist:** Before dying, Orestes reveals the truth: *"Ascension is not salvation, brother! It is a cosmic scalpel! When the Gate opens, it will tear the throat out of the Star Sea! We are the butchers!"*

---

### Mission 10: *Threshold of Light*
- **Location:** The Event Horizon of the Ascension Gate (The Eye of the Void).
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad.
- **Primary Objective:** Defend the three Ring Pylons of the Ascension Gate while Conductor Sola weaves the synchronization chord.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Multi-front assault. Acero dreadnoughts arrive from the west; Free Tide galleons strike from the east. The Gate pulses with energy, buffing all frames within its radiance with infinite Action Points for single turns.
- **Scripted Climax:** Mirei breaks formation: *"I can hear them calling. The ones who fell. Do not look back, Kaelen."* She triggers a full resonant overload, obliterating the lead Acero dreadnought at the cost of her physical existence.

---

### Mission 11: *The Dying Song*
- **Location:** The Threshold Interior.
- **Starting Squad:** Persistent Squad (Depleted).
- **Primary Objective:** Hold the perimeter for 10 minutes as the Gate reaches 100% resonance.
- **Tactical Dynamics:** Maximum difficulty. The Calm wavefront reaches the edge of the Gate itself. The battlefield shrinks by 10% each turn. The player must coordinate suicidal holding actions to buy seconds for the *Cradle*.

---

### Mission 12: *Beyond the Veil* (Campaign Climax)
- **Location:** Inside the Transcendent Rift.
- **Starting Squad:** Kaelen-7 (*Eidolon Prime Transcendent*) + The Conductor.
- **Primary Objective:** Defeat the cosmic manifestation of the False Vacuum Collapse (The Entity of Silence) to allow the surviving Cradles to pass beyond physical reality.
- **Climactic Resolution:**
  - Kaelen burns his own neural link to maximum output, fighting inside the surreal topology of the rift.
  - With the Entity defeated, the remaining Vinculados slip through the Gate into an eternal, bodiless realm of pure consciousness.
  - Behind them, the Gate implodes: the vacuum collapse seals the breach, leaving the physical universe scarred with a colossal dead zone that permanently cuts off all remaining current between the stars.
