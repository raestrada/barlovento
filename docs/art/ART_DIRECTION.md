# BARLOVENTO — Art Direction

This is the art bible. Every image generated by agents must obey it. The
prompts that produce the art are versioned under [`prompts/`](prompts/) and are
the **source of truth**: an asset is a rendering of its prompt, not the other
way around.

## Guiding principle

**Coherence over novelty.** The three factions must read as three answers to
one world — the same *sea*, the same *light*, three philosophies. Never invent
a new look per-asset; always derive from the tokens below.

## Global visual language

- **Medium:** painterly sci-fi concept art, cinematic lighting, volumetric
  depth. Not flat/cartoon, not photorealistic.
- **Palette:** cold, deep-space base (near-black blue/indigo) with one warm
  accent (ember orange) for energy and one cold accent (cyan) for the Éther.
- **Light:** strong single key light (a distant star) + rim light; heavy
  chiaroscuro; ships read clearly in silhouette.
- **Scale:** always communicate mass — small ships near large ships, scale
  reference (people, fighters, gantries) where relevant.
- **The Éther:** visible as faint cyan/silver currents and sails, never as
  flames.

## Per-faction identity (canonical)

| Faction | Shapes | Materials | Accent | Mood |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Armada of Acero** | angular, brutalist, massed | dark gunmetal, riveted plate | ember orange (thrusters/energy) | disciplined, industrial |
| **Vinculados** | sleek, organic-curved, humanoid frames | white ceramic + dark sinew, exposed core | cold magenta/violet (soul link) | elegant, transhuman |
| **Free Tide** | wooden hulls, sails, ropes, brass | wood, brass, canvas | warm amber + cyan sails | romantic, weathered |

These tokens are formalized in
[`prompts/000-global-style.md`](prompts/000-global-style.md) and reused verbatim
in every prompt.

## Consistency rules

1. Always include the global style tokens (or reference the file's token
   block) in a generation prompt.
2. Always include the faction identity block for faction assets.
3. Record every generated asset in the relevant faction/asset prompt file
   (filename, version, date, one-line note) so the registry stays authoritative.
4. Re-generate against the same prompt for variants; **edit the prompt and bump
   the version** when the look itself must change — never silently diverge.
5. Keep aspect ratios stable per asset class: splash/key art `16:9`, ship
   profiles `1:1` (or `4:3`), sprites/UI `1:1`.

## Output location

Generated images are copied into `assets/` following `assets/README.md`. The
prompt registry (in `docs/art/prompts/`) is committed; images are committed
only when they are final.
