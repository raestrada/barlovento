# Campaign Act Briefing Key Art Prompts

This document defines the canonical prompts for the key Act Briefing panoramic illustrations (16:9) across all three factions.

---

## The Armada of Acero

### Act I Briefing — Defense of Sluice Station Well-09 (`assets/briefings/acero_act1.png` / `assets/factions/acero_stage1_briefing.png`)
- **Focus:** Line-of-battle formation defending industrial sluice gate in cyan Éther delta against corsair raiders.

### Act II Briefing — The Siege of the Obsidian Sluice Line (`assets/briefings/acero_act2.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, heavy industrial chiaroscuro, cinematic lighting.
- **Palette:** Cold deep-space indigo, molten ember-orange plasma arcs, stark industrial floodlights, faint cyan Éther stream.
- **Subject:** Acero heavy siege fleet deployed across the basalt trenches of the Obsidian Sluice. In the foreground, a Crucible-Class Heavy Siege Monitor anchors with reinforced stabilizer clamps, discharging a massive curving arc of molten orange plasma from its spinal siege mortar toward a fortified corsair redoubt. Anvil Dreadnoughts and Hammer Battlecruisers advance in disciplined echelon behind it, angling their heavy bow armor plates against incoming fire.

### Act III Briefing — Apex of the Calm // The Final Dam (`assets/briefings/acero_act3.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, apocalyptic grandeur, cinematic rim lighting.
- **Palette:** Pitch black fading void of The Calm, fiery furnace ember-orange, blinding cyan spinal railgun flashes.
- **Subject:** The supreme fleet bastion of Acero at the threshold of the Calm Singularity. The colossal Colossus-Class Juggernaut *CNS Imperator* looms like an insurmountable steel fortress, firing quad heavy spinal railguns into the collapsing event horizon. Flanked by the Arsenal Carrier *CNS Forge-01* deploying flak screens and escorts, forming the final unyielding dam of steel to hold back cosmic extinction.

## The Vinculados

### Act I Briefing — Defense of Sanctuary Spire Psalm-4 (`assets/briefings/vinculados_act1.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, real-robot zero-G choreography, Gundam aesthetic, volumetric nebular glow.
- **Palette:** Deep indigo void, luminous cyan Éther river, soft violet and magenta soul-resonance pulses, pristine white ceramic armor with cobalt blue accents.
- **Subject:** Cradle Carrier Ark *Sanktuario Albor* with twin forward launch decks glowing with cyan electromagnetic rails, launching a 3-unit echelon platoon of athletic humanoid mobile suits (Eidolon and Anima frames) into zero-G flight. The mechas angle forward with legs swept back trailing cyan vernier particle plumes, holding beam carbines and beveled shields as they patrol between levitating harmonic crystal spires of Sanctuary Psalm-4.

### Act II Briefing — The Crystal Chasm Dive (`assets/briefings/vinculados_act2.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, dramatic verticality, dive-bomber vector dynamics.
- **Palette:** Deep shadowy basalt trench, brilliant cyan and electric violet energy lances, cold space indigo.
- **Subject:** High-altitude celestial vantage point looking down into the deep Basalt Crystal Chasm. Prototype Nous and Psyche combat frames execute a steep synchronized vertical dive from the upper celestial ledge (+16m) directly toward clumsy Acero capital ships navigating the tight canyon floor below. The lead Nous frame wields a blazing cyan resonance beam lance crackling with purple lightning, carving through the armored upper deck of an enemy cruiser.

### Act III Briefing — Transcendence Nexus // Seraph Mobile Armor (`assets/briefings/vinculados_act3.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, transcendental scale, Mobile Armor majesty.
- **Palette:** Swirling cyan and violet Éther vortex, pulsing magenta soul reactor core, cold deep-space backdrop.
- **Subject:** The colossal Mobile Armor *Seraph-Prime* entering the Transcendence Nexus at the Calm Singularity. Its massive swept white-and-cobalt shield binder wings fan out in seraphic form, launching a cloud of autonomous fin funnels that emit geometric lattices of laser fire, while its twin heavy spinal mega-beam cannons discharge continuous pillars of cyan energy into the dying void. An escorting choir of mobile suits flies in tight diamond formation around the leviathan.

## The Free Tide (Marea Libre)

### Act I Briefing — Running the Basalt Narrows (`assets/briefings/marea_act1.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, high-seas nautical adventure in space, dynamic motion blur and volumetric spray.
- **Palette:** Deep indigo void, radiant cyan Éther rapids (*El Río del Viento*), warm polished brass and mahogany timber, glowing amber stern lanterns.
- **Subject:** Corsair flagship void-galleon *Viento Rojo* and void-brigantine *Brisa Negra* heeling dramatically as they surf a high-velocity cyan river of Éther through the treacherous Basalt Narrows. Massive semi-rigid cyan quantum membrane sails billow from towering brass masts, banks of mechanical glowing energy oars sweep along the hulls, and spray of cosmic Éther particles kicks up from the bow as they break an Acero blockade.

### Act II Briefing — Raid on Foundry Complex Vulcan-3 (`assets/briefings/marea_act2.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, industrial guerrilla ambush, heavy chiaroscuro.
- **Palette:** Molten orange foundry glow, dark metallic refinery silhouettes, luminous cyan sails and muzzle flashes.
- **Subject:** Corsair strike squadron (quantum frigate *Sirena Libre* and fast raider *La Descarada*) weaving between the colossal industrial gantries and molten slag funnels of Acero Foundry Vulcan-3. The corsair ships maneuver under close-hauled quantum sails to evade heavy imperial railgun turrets, unleashing rolling port broadsides into refinery fuel silos that detonate in fiery orange explosions.

### Act III Briefing — The Maelstrom of Barlovento & The Void Leviathan (`assets/briefings/marea_act3.png`)
- **Ratio:** 16:9
- **Style:** Painterly sci-fi concept art, epic naval battle inside a cosmic tempest, Barlovento storm surfing.
- **Palette:** Blinding golden-orange solar prominences of the Great Sol Corona, swirling turquoise and cyan tidal currents, gleaming brass and dark iron.
- **Subject:** The colossal Corsair flagship *El Gran Leviatán* leading the entire Free Tide armada into the heart of the Maelstrom of Barlovento. The massive 6-sail void leviathan surfs the vortex crest with all quantum sails taut, catching the stellar wind to claim the Weather-Gauge [BARLOVENTO]. Its twin deck bombards and massive 3-tier broadside batteries fire synchronized salvos of plasma chain-shot, tearing through the imperial dreadnought line against the backdrop of the swirling solar corona.
