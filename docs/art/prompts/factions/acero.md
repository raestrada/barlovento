# Faction — The Armada of Acero (steel)

## Identity block (include in every Acero prompt)

```
FACTION: The Armada of Acero — a hard-science space navy in the spirit of
Battlestar Galactica / Star Wars.
SHAPES: angular, brutalist, massed, heavy capital ships with escort fighters.
MATERIALS: dark gunmetal, riveted armor plate, exposed heat-sinks.
ACCENT: ember-orange thrusters and energy conduits.
MOOD: disciplined, industrial, militaristic; line-of-battle formations.
```

## Doctrinal notes for generation

- Capital ships are broad, slow and heavily armored; escorts are small and
  darting. Show the mass contrast.
- No sails, no organic curves. Everything looks engineered and serviceable.
- Armor is heaviest on the bow; flanks and stern are thinner. Read the
  silhouette as a wall of steel meant to fight head-on.

## Hero visuals (planned)

  scale reference); brass, service stripes.
- **Strike Ace** — flight-suited pilot beside a Wasp; helmet, hoses.
