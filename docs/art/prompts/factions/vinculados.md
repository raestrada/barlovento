# Faction — The Vinculados (soul)

## Identity block (include in every Vinculados prompt)

```
FACTION: The Vinculados — real-robot mecha squadrons and carrier arks in the spirit of classic Gundam.
SHAPES: athletic geometric humanoid mobile suits in 3-unit wings, and catamaran Pegasus-class carrier arks with twin forward catapult decks and spinal mega-beam cannons.
MATERIALS: pristine white ceramic armor, cobalt blue chest and shoulder plates, dark slate inner mechanical frames, warm amber intake vents, glowing magenta soul cores.
ACCENT: luminous cyan for sensor visors, vernier plumes, and beam weapons; cold magenta/violet for internal soul-core resonance.
MOOD: retro-futuristic anime military mecha meets mystical Éther resonance; dynamic zero-G vector flight.
```

## Doctrinal notes for generation

- Mobile suits deploy in **agile 3-unit wings** (1 Lead Ace with 2 escorting wingmen in echelon formation).
- Always show suits in **dynamic zero-G space-flight posture**: torso angled forward into the thrust vector, knees bent, lower legs and feet swept backwards trailing glowing cyan vernier thruster plumes, holding beveled combat shields and beam carbines.
- The mothership is the **Cradle-Class Carrier Ark** (inspired by the classic White Base / SCV-70 Pegasus archetype): twin forward catamaran launch decks with glowing magnetic catapult runways, an elevated central conning bridge tower, and a massive central spinal mega-beam cannon.
- **Distinct from copyright:** do not use Bandai's trademark V-fin and red chin goatee. Instead, use the canonical **Harmonic Crest** (twin swept-back aerodynamic white antenna blades on the brow with a central cyan sensor optic) and integrated chest Soul Core.
