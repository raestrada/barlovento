# Faction — The Free Tide (wind)

## Identity block (include in every Free Tide prompt)

```
FACTION: The Free Tide — steampunk corsairs who sail the star-sea, in the
spirit of the Sankuokai sailing-ship spacecraft and Miyazaki's Porco Rosso.
SHAPES: space-faring vessels that EVOKE sailing ships but are re-engineered
for vacuum — sealed curved hulls, radial spar booms, LARGE semi-rigid
éther-sail membranes at solar-sail scale (much bigger than the hull), and
banks of articulated éther-oars along the flanks for maneuvering.
MATERIALS: treated/encapsulated timber, brass and riveted copper, glass
viewports, glowing sail membranes, lantern-banks of hab-light.
ACCENT: warm amber hab-light (life) against cold cyan éther sails and
oar-vane tips.
MOOD: romantic, weathered, handmade — yet believably spaceworthy; never a
literal wooden boat floating in a vacuum.
```

## Doctrinal notes for generation

- The icon is a **spaceworthy sailing ship**: a galleon/brigantine silhouette
  re-engineered for vacuum — not a metal rocket, and not a literal wooden boat.
- No open decks, oars, wheelhouses, or flapping cloth sails; nothing that
  implies water or gravity. Hulls are sealed and pressurized.
- "Sails" are **semi-rigid glowing membranes** on spar booms, catching the
  éther gradient — not canvas that billows. They must be **LARGE** (solar-sail
  scale, many times the hull's area): the éther gradient is faint, so sail
  area buys thrust.
- **Éther-oars:** banks of articulated spars tipped with small glowing cyan
  vanes that sweep the field, recalling a galley's oars but clearly
  mechanical — the maneuvering and emergency propulsion (especially when
  becalmed). Never literal wooden oars dipping in water.
- "Rigging" is functional: conduit lines, sensor spars, sail-furling booms.
- Cannon are **brass-cased mass-drivers/field-lances**, not muzzleloaders.
- Show life: warm amber light in viewports and lantern-banks, brass maneuvering
  jets and docking collars.
