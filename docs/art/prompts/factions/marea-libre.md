# Faction — The Free Tide (wind)

## Identity block (include in every Free Tide prompt)

```
FACTION: The Free Tide — steampunk corsairs who sail the star-sea, in the
spirit of the Sankuokai sailing-ship spacecraft and Miyazaki's Porco Rosso.
SHAPES: wooden sailing-ship hulls in space, sails, ropes, brass fittings.
MATERIALS: weathered wood, brass, canvas sails, glass lanterns.
ACCENT: warm amber lantern-light and cyan éther sails that catch the wind.
MOOD: romantic, weathered, adventurous; artisanal and handmade.
```

## Doctrinal notes for generation

- The icon is the **space sailing ship**: a galleon/brigantine silhouette with
  éther sails, rigging and brass — not a metal rocket.
- Show the wind: currents, sail billow, drifting debris. Movement comes from
  the sea, not engines.
