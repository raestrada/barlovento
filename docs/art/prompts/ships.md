# Ships

Reference concept art for ship models. Compose every prompt from the global
style tokens in `000-global-style.md` and the faction identity block in
`factions/<name>.md`. These images are visual guides for the procedural 3D
models built in `scripts/entities/ship_model.gd`.

## Acero dreadnought (v1)

```
STYLE: painterly sci-fi concept art, cinematic lighting, volumetric depth, not flat, not cartoon, not photorealistic.
PALETTE: cold deep-space base of near-black blue and indigo; warm ember-orange accent for energy; cold cyan accent for the Éther.
LIGHT: strong single key light from a distant star, strong rim light, heavy chiaroscuro, clear readable ship silhouettes.
QUALITY: high detail, clean composition, no text, no watermark, no logo.

FACTION: The Armada of Acero — a hard-science space navy in the spirit of Battlestar Galactica / Star Wars.
SHAPES: angular, brutalist, massed, heavy capital ship.
MATERIALS: dark gunmetal, riveted armor plate, exposed heat-sinks.
ACCENT: ember-orange thrusters and energy conduits.

ASSET: a single heavy Acero dreadnought starship, 3/4 profile view, isolated on a dark background. Long tapered slab hull with a wedge-shaped armored bow, a raised blocky citadel/bridge amidships, rows of side turret batteries, twin engine nacelles at the stern with glowing ember-orange thrusters, a dorsal fin, riveted gunmetal plating. Tiny fighters as scale reference nearby. No other capital ships.
RATIO: 16:9
```
