# Environments

Environment backdrops and space vistas for BARLOVENTO. Compose every environment from the
global style tokens in `000-global-style.md`.

## Space Backdrop (v1)

```
STYLE: painterly sci-fi concept art, cinematic lighting, volumetric depth, not flat, not cartoon, not photorealistic.
PALETTE: cold deep-space base of near-black blue and indigo; warm ember-orange accent for energy; cold cyan accent for the Éther.
LIGHT: strong single key light from a distant star, strong rim light, heavy chiaroscuro, clear readable ship silhouettes.
ÉTHER: faint cyan/silver currents and sails, never flames.
QUALITY: high detail, clean composition, no text, no watermark, no logo.

ASSET: a vast empty deep-space background for a space tactics game, no ships and no planets. A dark indigo-black void filled with a dense field of distant stars, faint wispy cyan éther currents threading through the scene, and a very subtle deep-blue and violet nebula haze near the edges. Seamless ambient backdrop, atmospheric depth, mostly empty in the center.

RATIO: 16:9
```
