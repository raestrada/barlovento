# BARLOVENTO

A 3D turn-based space strategy game in the spirit of *Homeworld* — real spatial
depth, naval inertia and mass — played in turns instead of real time.

> **Barlovento** (nautical, "windward") is the advantageous position a ship
> fights to take before battle: the *weather gauge*. That fight for position is
> exactly the game's strategic half.

## One-line pitch

Fight for the wind in a dying star-sea. Three civilizations — one of steel, one
of soul, one of wind — race to save or escape a universe whose dark-energy
"wind" is running out.

## Status

Pre-production. Design and tooling are being bootstrapped for 100% LLM-agent
development. There is no playable build yet.
