## One battle, two minds.

**BARLOVENTO** is a space strategy game built on a kind of battle that has
never existed before: a war you fight **twice**, with two different minds.

First you think like an **admiral** — reading the sea, committing your fleet
to course and formation, racing the enemy for the windward advantage. Then you
think like an **ace** — turn by turn, ship by ship, fighting your own momentum
for every meter of the void.

Most strategy games make you choose: the grand view or the kill. BARLOVENTO
makes you master both. It is not one loop with a gimmick bolted on. It is two
complete games, welded into a single, escalating duel.

## Hold the wind, or lose the sea.

Beyond every world lies the **Éther** — the faint, navigable current that turns
the void into an ocean. For centuries it was everywhere: cheap, endless,
unquestioned. Now a wall of stillness, **the Calm**, is spreading across it.
Where it passes, the current dies. Ships fall silent. Stars go dark.

Three powers answer the same catastrophe in three ways that cannot coexist.

## Three Ways. One dying sea.

| | **The Armada of Acero** | **The Vinculados** | **The Free Tide** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Creed** | Steel | Soul | Wind |
| **They move by** | Mass and formation | Reflex and duel | The current itself |
| **The Calm costs them** | Their empire's fuel | Their very identity | Their motion |

- **The Armada of Acero** forged an empire on the sea's energy. When the sea
  dies, the empire starves — so they will dam and command what remains.
- **The Vinculados** bound their minds to the sea. When it dies, their souls
  go silent — so they will leave it, whatever that takes.
- **The Free Tide** sail the sea itself. When it dies, they stop — so they
  will wake it again, whatever that costs the others.

Everyone is guilty. No one is entirely wrong.

## The signature: plan, then fight

**The Approach.** You and your enemy issue fleet orders at the same time.
Course. Thrust. Formation. Facing. Then the approach unfolds in one sweeping
pass — a cinematic collision of two plans, neither side knowing what the other
committed until it is already too late. It does not win the battle. It decides
where the cards fall.

**The Engagement.** Turn by turn, you command ships against their own
momentum. Burn engines to change vector. Ride the drift. Turn armor into the
fire. Your hand-picked squad of aces fights with a precision the line ships
cannot match.

A perfect plan can still lose to a flawless pilot. A flawless pilot still
needs a fleet behind them. This is the tension at the heart of BARLOVENTO, and
no other game has it.

## Every ship fights its own momentum

There are no waypoints here. No instant turns. A capital ship commits minutes
before impact; an ace dances on drift. **Inertia is not a statistic — it is
the game.** Learn to master it, and the sea answers to you.

## Features

- **Two-phase combat** — simultaneous fleet planning, then turn-based tactics.
- **Fully 3D battles** with a free camera and true vector movement.
- **Three asymmetric Ways**, each with its own ships, heroes and relationship
  to the dying sea.
- **A squad of aces** you raise, arm, and can lose forever.
- **The Calm** — a living strategic front that reshapes the map, the economy,
  and the stakes of every campaign.
- **Three endings.** One sea. No truce.
